FAQ

Pricing questions

What is a credit (✦)?

1✦ = one AI sort. Alfredo reads your dump, splits it into tasks, feelings, and ponderings, and suggests priorities — that whole pass costs one credit. Everything else — dumping, boards, calendar — is free and needs no credits.

How much can I use for free?

Verify your email and you get 20 free credits, refilled to 20✦ on the 1st of every month. No credit card required. If you sort about once a day, the free plan is plenty.

I already have my own API key

Connect a Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini API key (BYOK) and AI sorting uses no credits at all. Your key is stored only in your browser and never sent to our servers. See the guide for setup.

How does payment work?

Purchases happen inside the extension on the Credits & Subscription page, processed securely by Paddle. You can cancel Pro anytime, and unused credits roll over to the next month.
